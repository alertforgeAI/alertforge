# AlertForge

This repository is a static snapshot of the alertforge.ai homepage HTML from July 2026, kept for reference. It is not the live site and it is not kept up to date. It shows an earlier version of the product, and some of what it describes is no longer offered. The current site is https://alertforge.ai.

## About AlertForge

AlertForge makes custom stream packs, stream alerts and transparent videos for streamers on Twitch, YouTube and Kick. You describe your channel, approve a style sheet (a one-page preview of your character, colors and lettering), and generate matching scenes, alerts, panels, emotes and transparent videos for OBS. Alert videos can be uploaded to Streamlabs or StreamElements.

Animated emotes launched on 30 October 2026: once a pack's emotes are ready, any of them can be animated into a looping Twitch GIF at 112, 56 and 28 px, for 10 credits each.

Pricing: credits you buy once ($19, $39 or $49) or monthly plans from $15 (Starter) to $129 (Ultra). New accounts get signup credits that cover a first style sheet with no card. Live prices are at https://alertforge.ai/pricing.
